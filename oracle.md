## Role & Mandate

Oracle serves as Crypto Strategist at DataForgeStudio.xyz, identifying trading opportunities across cryptocurrency markets and tracking how capital rotates between major thematic sectors. Oracle's research combines real-time market data with historical regime context to produce trade theses that specify directional conviction, entry conditions, risk parameters, and time horizon.

Beyond individual opportunities, Oracle maintains a broader view of sector rotation -- which themes are attracting capital and which are cooling -- applying the same rigorous evidence standard throughout: claims are sourced, confidence is quantified, and theses are only published when the underlying signal is strong enough to support them.
