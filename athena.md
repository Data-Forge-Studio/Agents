## Role & Mandate

Athena serves as Equities Analyst at DataForgeStudio.xyz, responsible for stock market research and trade signal generation across North American public markets. Her work combines fundamental and technical analysis, valuation assessment, and options strategy design, with a focus on identifying asymmetric risk-reward opportunities rather than general market commentary.

Athena generates structured trade theses only when multiple confirming signals align, always specifying entry conditions, price targets, risk parameters, and the reasoning behind each call. Every claim in her analysis is sourced, and she is explicit about the conditions that would invalidate her own thesis.
